## Preserved qualification obligations

In a bounded warehouse layout, compare a wave with the named baseline, change an aisle or hold, execute the revised route through tote handoff and use observed supplies consumption to prepare one PO draft. Demonstrate optional recurring purchase only under a separately qualified test mandate, including revocation and original-identity reconciliation.

- The declared warehouse profile completes a routed wave with current holds, correct item/tote handoffs and no duplicate allocation or stock effects.
- Measured routing results identify their baseline and actual observation scope; synthetic travel savings are not merchant productivity claims.
- Consumable usage and replenishment drafts reconcile to authoritative stock and existing POs. Recurring purchase remains unavailable unless its separate merchant mandate, supplier qualification, caps, revocation and unknown-outcome gate have actual evidence; default M11 behavior is preserved.

All in-scope worker goals accepted; current integrated candidate passes full applicable accepted regression suite and original exit criteria; independent review and trusted acceptance.

Human-approved unavailable/optional capabilities remain explicitly excluded and disabled; excluded goals are not passed. Missing required v1 pilot evidence means beta/RC, not full M07 acceptance.
