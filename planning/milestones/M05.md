## Required integrated demonstration

Resolve a tracking query, process an approved return, hand off an exception and disable the model while the operator continues the same case.

## Original exit criteria

- Human takeover stops further agent actions without losing case context; new messages in other channels do not bypass customer verification.
- Verified policy answers and completed business actions are measured separately from attempts; all sampled financial outcomes reconcile.
- Provider outage or budget exhaustion falls back to queued/manual operation; no new provider receives customer data without merchant approval.
- Opt-out arriving concurrently with a scheduled marketing message suppresses the send; transactional behavior follows configured consent/policy rules.

## Evidence gate

All in-scope worker goals accepted; current integrated candidate passes full applicable accepted regression suite and original exit criteria; independent review and trusted acceptance.

Human-approved unavailable/optional capabilities remain explicitly excluded and disabled; excluded goals are not passed. Missing required v1 pilot evidence means beta/RC, not full M07 acceptance.

A prior accepted component does not prove the integrated candidate still works. Re-run current applicable accepted tests, introduced invariants, affected connected-provider qualifications and the complete milestone journey.
