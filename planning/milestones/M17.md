# M17 — Multimodal evidence assistance

Introduce an explicitly opt-in image-first evidence-assistance path only after qualifying intake, authorized model access, and the full privacy lifecycle. Keep audio/video disabled unless a named additional use case and its media pipeline are separately declared and qualified.

## Required integrated demonstration

Opt into the restricted image path, upload and approve a usable image, record an evidence-assistance observation, handle an ambiguous/oversized case, erase evidence while processing is in flight, and inspect the exact supported/deferred modality matrix.

## Original exit criteria

- The default attachment boundary remains restrictive, and every enabled image path has explicit intake, quarantine/processing, exact-case access, provider-purpose, and privacy lifecycle evidence.
- Image observations are evidence-linked, bounded, reviewable, and independent of authenticity, eligibility, approval, physical receipt, and financial authority.
- Any model-quality claim uses a cohort and gate defined before actual media evaluation; fixture-only implementation status remains distinct from qualification.
- Audio/video support is either explicitly disabled/deferred or tied to its own implemented, opted-in, and actually measured profile; an image-only release does not claim all-modality support.
- Affected intake/access/privacy/adapter checks and the declared qualification cohort provide candidate-bound evidence without repeating the full product regression suite per goal.

## Evidence gate

All in-scope worker goals accepted; current integrated candidate passes full applicable accepted regression suite and original exit criteria; independent review and trusted acceptance.

Human-approved unavailable/optional capabilities remain explicitly excluded and disabled; excluded goals are not passed. Missing required v1 pilot evidence means beta/RC, not full M07 acceptance.

A prior accepted component does not prove the integrated candidate still works. Re-run current applicable accepted tests, introduced invariants, affected connected-provider qualifications and the complete milestone journey.
