## Preserved qualification obligations

- A declared text campaign produces source-grounded variants with reviewable immutable approval and correct suppression under changing facts.
- Private or unqualified source data, invented claims, stale offers and post-approval content changes cannot reach the delivery path.
- The evidence separates content quality, authorized sends, attribution and causal effect; any live commercial claim has its own permitted observations.
