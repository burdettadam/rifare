## Preserved qualification obligations

Through a specifically qualified M17 image profile, process one readable and one unreadable synthetic return-evidence image on the named desktop, show typed client-reported assistance, delete or revoke a pending source and continue manually with no remote fallback or business effect.

- The applicable M17 media/intake/privacy profile is enabled only with its required evidence, and the declared browser vision companion/API actually processes authorized image versions with the complete decision head.
- The frozen image corpus, named device/runtime/companion manifest and complete results establish only the published image-assistance scope; text or hosted demonstrations do not substitute.
- Wrong-case/version, decode limits, missing media, late completion, correction/erasure, device failure and destination fallback preserve source privacy, client-provenance limits and manual continuity.
- No client image result authorizes routing or business effects, and no audio/video or broader authenticity claim is enabled. Missing M17, vision or actual-browser evidence remains open in formal assessment.

All in-scope workers, exact original criteria and formal dependencies require actual applicable evidence; the current integrated candidate must satisfy its separately authorized integration/release checks, independent review and trusted acceptance.

Unavailable artifacts, hardware, upstream support, media/privacy foundations or required observed qualification remain explicit blockers. Optional scope can be excluded only by a trusted human decision with a reduced truthful claim; exclusion is not a passing result.
