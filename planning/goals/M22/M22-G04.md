## Preserved acceptance criteria

- An intervening purchase, opt-out, source correction or material offer change suppresses or returns the affected variant for review.
- Duplicate schedules and retries retain the same logical delivery identity and do not multiply campaign exposure.
- A stale variant is never silently regenerated and sent under its old approval; unaffected approved variants may proceed within existing campaign rules.
