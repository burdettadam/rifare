# M21-G05 — Prepare supplies replenishment and gate recurring purchases

**Milestone:** M21 · **Type:** worker_goal · **Lane:** warehouse_intelligence · **Risk:** critical

**State:** not started · **Owner:** unassigned · **Execution:** local_then_separate_qualification

> Planning specification only. No application test, provider operation or feature completion is claimed.

## Outcome

Default to threshold/scheduled PO drafts that account for stock and open orders; optionally execute recurring purchases only through a separately activated, bounded merchant mandate and qualified supplier.

[M21-G04](../M21/M21-G04.md); [M11-G04](../M11/M11-G04.md); [M11-G05](../M11/M11-G05.md); [M11-G06](../M11/M11-G06.md); [M11-G07](../M11/M11-G07.md); [M08-G99](../M08/M08-G99.md); [M11-G99](../M11/M11-G99.md)

Dependencies must be **accepted**, not merely assigned or implemented. Inspect current accepted evidence before starting.

## Bounded ownership

- Versioned threshold and schedule configuration
- One logical replenishment request or mandate occurrence per trigger
- Existing PO, budget, approval and original-identity reconciliation integration
- Optional mandate contract for exact merchant, supplier account, allowed SKUs, quantity limits, currency, per-order and aggregate spend caps, validity and revocation
- Version-bound mandate approval by a principal with M11-G06 procurement authority, with the existing separation of duties
