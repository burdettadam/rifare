## Bounded ownership

- Inventory the current M17 implementation and inherited intake surfaces before adding image controls. Existing model_access:false, attachment rejection, scan/quarantine and retention limits remain effective until their own owner explicitly qualifies the image path.
- Select one bounded image-first return-evidence use case, supported formats and request limits, current actor/tenant/case purpose and exact media-version authorization. Declare audio and video unsupported in this milestone.
- Reuse M17's exact-content upload/grant and storage/deletion contracts. If no attachment exists, keep the input unavailable or use newly prepared synthetic fixtures through those contracts; do not fabricate an existing attachment or create an ungoverned upload path.
- Specify whether approved local-only user-selected images may be previewed without upload. Such bytes remain a local draft/client assertion until separately authorized M17 intake establishes a server-linked media record; no implicit attachment registration or external upload occurs.
