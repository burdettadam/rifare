# M15-G03 — Fence replay, revocation and active work across verifier processes

**Milestone:** M15 · **Type:** worker_goal · **Lane:** security · **Risk:** critical

**State:** not started · **Owner:** unassigned · **Execution:** local_then_separate_qualification

> Planning specification only. No application test, provider operation or feature completion is claimed.

## Outcome

Make proof replay and current task restrictions effective across concurrent requests, restarts and lease renewal.

## Dependencies

[M15-G02](../M15/M15-G02.md); [M02-G20](../M02/M02-G20.md); [M02-G21](../M02/M02-G21.md); [M02-G99](../M02/M02-G99.md); [M07-G99](../M07/M07-G99.md)

Dependencies must be **accepted**, not merely assigned or implemented. Inspect current accepted evidence before starting.

## Bounded ownership

- Add bounded shared replay admission with an atomic uniqueness decision for the appropriate issuer/sender/proof identifier, retaining entries through the accepted proof window and skew allowance.
- Fail closed when replay admission or required current-authority reads are unavailable. Do not fall back to an instance-local Map or silently clear the active replay window on restart.
- Reuse current parent-session, membership, policy, configuration, stop and lease-generation checks. Token lifetime, lease renewal and client handoff cannot exceed the original task ceiling or recover revoked scope.
- Record minimized denial/admission reasons and references to the current actor, original principal and task. Never persist raw access tokens, proof JWTs or private keys in receipts.
- Preserve existing unknown-operation reconciliation semantics: retrying authentication or taking over a lease must not create a fresh identity for an already attempted business effect.

Additive specification from the separately retained roadmap. Concrete assignment and allowed paths require a prior maintainer record; only M13 is assigned by ADR-0042.
