12. Complete applicable existing target-stack, integration-readiness, independent security and formal acceptance obligations separately. Current SQLite diagnostic success cannot establish PostgreSQL, production availability or a fully accepted M07/M08 release.

## Preserved acceptance criteria

- The evidence manifest identifies candidate/configuration, selected cases, results and omissions, and explicitly records focused checks as neither full regression nor goal acceptance.
- A synthetic-only result is labeled local development readiness. A live-qualified label requires observed behavior on the named actual deployment, including direct origin and shared replay enforcement.
- Disabling or misconfiguring the remote profile denies its routes while existing authorized local/manual work remains usable; no implicit bearer or portal-only fallback appears.
- The release claim lists supported remote operations and unqualified transports/providers without changing the checkpoint's existing accepted-goal ledger.
