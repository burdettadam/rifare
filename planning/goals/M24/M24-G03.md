## Preserved acceptance criteria

- Both browser implementations accept the same declared logical task and merchant answer IDs, with no provider-specific branch in the base skill or business action contract.
- Invalid distributions, missing questions, reordered score meanings or unsupported response semantics remain unusable; result normalization never repairs a malformed answer into apparent confidence.
- Changing only a client-reported runtime/model label cannot promote a result to canonical M13 evidence or a qualified server route; observed and asserted facts remain distinct.
- A failed or revoked compact attempt retains its exact observation status and manual alternative, with no unapproved model/destination fallback and no business effect.
