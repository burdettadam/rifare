One question resource serves two existing presentation-field variants. Current
case/task/profile/skill authorization and exact source hashes bind bounded
incoming customer text and required order status. Missing/stale required evidence
and oversized complete history are unavailable without dispatch. Customer claims,
source facts and optional gaps remain distinct. Attachments and merchant-specific
authority overrides are excluded. Existing loaders, profile defaults and case
pins are unchanged. Root-owned native results and two independent decisions are
kept in the cumulative evidence; no connected or formal acceptance is inferred.
