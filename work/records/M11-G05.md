# M11-G05 — Supplier terms and immutable PO versions

State: implemented; root-owned native evidence and independent review pending.

Assigned by ADR-0040 from `b1e8be4a6a06ebe4289f95ba05630a13926e967a`, with one
writer in `goal/M11-procurement`. Formal M07-G99/M08-G99 and goal acceptance remain
blocked. External suppliers and actual commercial qualification are unavailable.

`SupplierPurchaseOrders` imports independent synthetic supplier terms and creates
immutable line/version/currency/cost/lead-time records. It preserves explicit
each/case conversions and both source identities. A creation is a draft; original
native sends, acknowledgments and cancellation events remain separate. Changes
invalidate unused approvals while prior versions remain readable. Unknown sends
retain their original identity and prohibit a new version/retry.

Tests and independent arithmetic were committed first at `8e3bfba`. Three G05
native cases assert exact totals, changed units/prices, revision invalidation and
an actual isolated initial-state fault that invents supplier acknowledgment.
Root alone executes the bounded native selection and final reviews.

Migration 043 contributes supplier/header/version tables alongside shared budget
and planning tables. Explicit privacy allowlists and restore revocation retain
business records without inventing customer identities. Original source and
unknown-operation history must survive rollback; no delete-and-replay recovery.

See `docs/PROCUREMENT.md` and the G05 contract for exact authenticated getter and
line schemas. No author product execution or real supplier-facing operation was
performed, and the author records no acceptance.
