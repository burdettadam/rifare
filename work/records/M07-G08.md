commerce SQLite installations to one separate coordinator. A durable immutable

change adds the fence to `packages/commerce/data-guards.mts`; prior recovery and
egress assertions remain intact. The integration owner owns any exact source-pin

checks that rejection creates no local binding or coordinator registration and
that the unrelated merchant can still write. Root-authorized preparation in the
new disposable fixture driver removes only other synthetic merchants, restores

changed by this test-input construction.

retains source IDs, case/configuration history, operation/receipt identity,
amounts, source-native references and current effect states. Cutover repeats that
comparison under the lock, rejects stale epochs, divergent histories, unresolved
effects and unfinished executions, then switches one owner. Rollback advances
the epoch and leaves both installations paused without replaying any operation.
There is no automatic rollback resume or imported-work activation.
