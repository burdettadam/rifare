# M11-G06 — Procurement approval and budget controls

State: implemented; root-owned native evidence and independent review pending.

ADR-0040 assigns G06 from `b1e8be4a6a06ebe4289f95ba05630a13926e967a`, with the
procurement author as sole writer. Formal prerequisite and external qualification
gates remain blocked; this work creates no actual supplier agreement or payment.

`ProcurementApprovals` uses existing kernel permissions/transactions, exact money
arithmetic, configured approval limits, independent reviewers and atomic budget
reservations. Drafting actors cannot approve their own version. Current authority,
resource/PO/supplier pins, expiry, budget and stop stamps are checked at the final
native boundary. A durable source binding outlives adapter instances; missing
current guards deny new effects while original committed results remain readable.

Unknown sends retain the full obligation and original operation ID. A new request
key cannot repeat the PO. Acknowledgment/cancellation are source observations,
while invoice/payment remain unobserved. Source cancellation does not invent a
refund or release the retained budget audit.

Test-first commit `8e3bfba` defines the three original G06 cases, PORT-031 and
the native boundary case. They cover self-approval, changed/expired obligations,
atomic competing reservations, partial receipt conservation, lost responses,
fresh-adapter guard denial and an actual isolated separation-of-duties fault.
All eleven author cases and all actual fault executions remain root-owned; the
author ran none. A twelfth root integration slot is preserved.

Privacy/restore disables approvals and guards even without customer tombstones,
preserving unknown command IDs and budget amounts. See `docs/PROCUREMENT.md` and
the G06 contract for methods, source constraints, rollback and remaining limits.
This work record is not an acceptance decision or independent review.
