  per-package eligibility, exact currency totals and current-source revalidation.

  synthetic source with fixed merchant identity and versioned package, account
  and rate records. No provider transport or account-creation operation.
- `tools/demo-carrier-quotes.mts`: disposable quote and affected-package stale
  demonstration; no credentials, customer data or external effects.

metadata are changed by this author. No persistent storage surface is added.

money primitives. Ephemeral snapshot integrity does not grant execution authority.
