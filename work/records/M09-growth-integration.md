## Privacy, restoration and source ownership

The additive schema contains six shopping/catalog, eleven marketing and six
optional-provider tables. All seven earlier storage contracts, their 186 table
definitions and classifications, the legacy 167 projection and the independent
170/178 snapshots are retained. A frozen protected-base 186-table fixture checks
the earlier classifications separately from the new 23-table extension.

`growth-privacy.mts` connects subject exports and allowlist minimization to the
canonical lifecycle. Marketing exports project only the requested subject and
relevant configurations, omit the assignment seed and bearer/session hashes, and
retain the original experiment denominator after erasure. Original cart, native
catalog, message, cancellation and optional-operation identities remain available
for audit without carrying permission to resend. Suppression replay rebuilds
allowlists even for already minimized copies and hash-only subject checkpoints.
Merchant import, snapshot restore and uninstall revoke the new approvals,
campaign schedules, optional consent and processor readiness locally.

Separate synthetic source stores are not erased by canonical database cleanup.
Optional deletion queues address the original source operation only. Published
merchant descriptions require reviewed source handling. Caller copies, database
pages and historical checkpoints retain the existing physical-deletion limits.
The M08 case of an unimported stock movement followed by subject erasure remains
an operator reconciliation limitation; this milestone adds no automatic repair.
