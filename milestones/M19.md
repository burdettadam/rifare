## Required integrated demonstration

For one qualified merchant, earn points on a two-line purchase, redeem an approved amount, partially return the original purchase, and reconcile the provider balance while injecting a duplicate event and lost acknowledgment.

## Original exit criteria

- The declared provider supports the demonstrated lifecycle with attributable source events and no duplicate accrual, redemption or reversal.
- Concurrent redemption, partial return, repeated exchange, timeout and insufficient-balance cases preserve original operation identity and instrument boundaries.
- Unsupported partner behavior and incomplete settlement remain visible; loyalty never supplies financial or messaging authority.
- The declared loyalty data lifecycle preserves customer erasure after late provider events and restore, with any permitted accounting retention explicitly minimized and recorded.

## Evidence gate

All in-scope worker goals accepted; current integrated candidate passes full applicable accepted regression suite and original exit criteria; independent review and trusted acceptance.

Human-approved unavailable/optional capabilities remain explicitly excluded and disabled; excluded goals are not passed. Missing required v1 pilot evidence means beta/RC, not full M07 acceptance.

A prior accepted component does not prove the integrated candidate still works. Re-run current applicable accepted tests, introduced invariants, affected connected-provider qualifications and the complete milestone journey.
