## Required integrated demonstration

Select an eligible drop-off for a two-item return, obtain a credential, accept only one item, reconcile duplicate and delayed events, and trace its observed consolidation/receipt without claiming the outstanding item's receipt or an unobserved refund.

## Original exit criteria

- One declared method/site/item configuration has actual connected and physical evidence for every claimed handoff stage.
- Partial intake, duplicate events, stale credentials, lost acknowledgments and missing custody do not fabricate goods, inspection or money movement.
- Shopper wording and release capability reflect actual packaging, partner availability and financial-trigger limitations.
