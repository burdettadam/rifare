## Required integrated demonstration

For one verified shopper, move from a status question to an exchange and an eligible subsequent shopping action across two channels; change runtime during an uncertain effect, reconcile it once, and show that opt-out prevents the later follow-up.

## Original exit criteria

- Two merchant profiles complete the declared text journey across two qualified channels using shared skills and existing business operations.
- Correction, deletion, revocation, opt-out, stale-source, runtime handover and unknown-outcome cases preserve both privacy and original receipt identity.
- The support matrix records every qualified action/caller/channel combination and labels missing external evidence without inferring success.
