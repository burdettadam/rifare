## Required integrated demonstration

Install from the release, replay one case on both platforms, switch its agent, restore a backup in isolation and import a merchant configuration without a code fork.

## Original exit criteria

- Clean install, upgrade and restore rehearsals succeed without vendor license-server or proprietary-agent dependency; restore cannot replay live money actions.
- All mandatory critical conformance scenarios pass, no unresolved critical/high release-blocking issues remain, and sampled financial/inventory records reconcile.
- Two platforms, two independent runtimes and supported model configurations have recorded evidence for the shared portability subset.
- Pilot outcomes meet predeclared quality thresholds and are approved by the maintainer plus merchant representatives. With insufficient live evidence, label the release beta/RC rather than silently waiving the gate.

## Evidence gate

All in-scope worker goals accepted; current integrated candidate passes full applicable accepted regression suite and original exit criteria; independent review and trusted acceptance.

Human-approved unavailable/optional capabilities remain explicitly excluded and disabled; excluded goals are not passed. Missing required v1 pilot evidence means beta/RC, not full M07 acceptance.

A prior accepted component does not prove the integrated candidate still works. Re-run current applicable accepted tests, introduced invariants, affected connected-provider qualifications and the complete milestone journey.
