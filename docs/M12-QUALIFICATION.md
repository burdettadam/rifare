# M12 agency and extension qualification assessment

## Decision boundary

M12 has a bounded local development assignment under ADR-0041. **Formal M12-G99
acceptance remains blocked.** The original M07-G99 prerequisite, accepted worker
decisions, independent contributor observations, human decisions and applicable
formal cumulative evidence are not supplied by synthetic local checks.

The original planning packets, goal dependencies, acceptance ledger, licensing
policy and regression requirements remain unchanged. A local integration decision
does not adopt a license, authorize contribution solicitation, enable code mode,
publish a release or represent an independently contributed community extension.

## Original demonstration and exit criteria

The original G99 demonstration is to install a **community extension**, review
its requested permissions, canary it in one store and revoke it without disabling
other merchants, with exact build/configuration, cumulative evidence, independent
reviews and maintainer decision. Public/tagged release requires human approval.

The local demonstration instead uses an explicitly synthetic mirrored instruction
fixture. It exercises real installer/release/permission/profile services on one
kernel, per-store template decisions, original case pins and isolated revocation.
That useful local evidence does not authenticate a community contributor.

The two original milestone exit criteria remain:

1. **An independent contributor ships a compatible extension without core forks
   or access to private infrastructure.** Blocked: attributable independent
   contribution, authorized public contribution terms and independently observed
   execution are missing.
2. **A centrally published template can be upgraded, rejected or rolled back per
   merchant without crossing tenant boundaries.** The assigned local presentation
   slice provides focused behavioral evidence; original prerequisite acceptance,
   independent intended-use qualification and formal cumulative evidence remain
   separate obligations.

No synthetic PREP case is registered as a completed external/human case. Original
G06, G07, G08, conditional runtime G09 and G99 cases remain unperformed for their
formal execution types. Requirement closure remains conjunctive for BUILD-082,
BUILD-092, SKILL-024, SKILL-025, EXP-013 and EXP-014.

## Focused verification and review

The planned root selection is 44 cases across four whole files: 12 agency,
14 extension including one local integration demonstration, nine assurance PREP,
and the retained nine privacy cases. Actual invocation reports and native XML/TAP
are in the checkpoint's ignored `evidence/development/` directory. Those reports,
not this planning count, establish what ran and its outcomes.

The unchanged development ceiling is 60 actual native cases, four whole files
and one total 60-second deadline per invocation. Authors and reviewers execute
zero product tests/imports/demos. Only a concrete failure or consequential change
justifies repeating affected whole files. G99 does not trigger full regression,
nested catalog, browser, controller, restoration or connected campaigns.

Two fresh nonauthor authority/security and regression decisions bind the exact
candidate and evidence, with at most three corrective rounds. Their actual
reports remain distinct from root's local nonrelease integration decision.
Any carried result identifies unchanged test bytes, the exact source delta and
both final review bindings. The cumulative handoff retains every actual run,
including failures and superseded candidate observations.

## Privacy and recovery

Eight new tenant/store tables have explicit storage handling. New grants,
templates, artifact reviews and transition/retirement records are excluded from
unrelated customer exports. Nested/scalar minimization is applied again to
restored copies and repeated hash-only erasure. Valid unrelated merchant history
and immutable original record identities remain.

Canonical import, recovery and uninstall revoke new authority even with no
customer tombstones. Inert archived records do not recreate memberships, sessions,
skill approvals or execution grants. Recovery remains quarantined; original
uncertain operation identity survives and is not blindly replayed. Separately
held mirrors, source copies, exports and caller memory retain their explicit
operator cleanup duties.

## Continuation

all Git refs/reachable history, source and the actual M12 evidence. Read its
