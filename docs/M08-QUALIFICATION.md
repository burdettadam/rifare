# M08 qualification assessment

**M08-G99 remains blocked.** The owner assigned its assessment as the fifth
entry in ADR-0037, following M08-G07 through G10. That assignment authorizes
bounded local development and a review of missing obligations. It does not
authorize a full milestone campaign or waive an original gate.

## Retained milestone requirements

The original milestone requires a warehouse to complete sale-to-shipment and
return-to-disposition cycles without unexplained inventory differences.
Duplicate scans, partial receipts, carrier timeouts and split shipments must
produce correct records and explicit exceptions. Its named demo is:

> Scan, pack and ship an edited order; receive its partial return and reconcile all stock movements.

Those are conjunctive requirements. The goal requires formal acceptance of
M08-G01 through G10 and M07-G99, a current integrated candidate, the full applicable
cumulative evidence, actual connected source records, independent authority and
regression review, and a separate trusted acceptance decision. The original
planning packets, criteria and accepted-goal ledger remain unchanged.

## Blocking obligations

1. M07-G99 and all formal M08 prerequisites remain unaccepted. The accepted ledger
   still contains only M00-G01 through G04 under their recorded historical scope.
2. No real warehouse/carrier accounts, approved connected configuration, physical
   observations or partner attestations are available for this batch. Separate
   synthetic source databases cannot establish that qualification.
3. Original `CASE-M08-G99-01`, `CASE-M08-G99-02` and `CASE-M08-G99-03` remain
   unperformed at their formal scope. No local preparation case replaces them.
4. The original edited-order milestone demo and current full applicable
   cumulative verification have not been performed as a milestone campaign.
5. Nonauthor reviews and the maintainer's local integration decision, when
   recorded for this candidate, assess only this bounded development scope.
   They cannot substitute for the preceding missing evidence.

No milestone acceptance, release, tag, hosted push, production operation, real
warehouse/carrier effect, paid source transaction or external contact is
authorized. M09 remains unassigned. The next development assignment must retain
these open gates rather than infer acceptance from local progress.
