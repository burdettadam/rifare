## What the lifecycle can and cannot establish

The current synthetic protocol can observe native redemption debits by original
operation identity and compute original-unit accrual/reversal targets. It cannot
prove that the provider issued this order's proposed earnings or reversed its
returned unit. An account-wide earned total is not an attributable order event.
A successful refund is evidence about money and does not close a loyalty gap.

Accordingly, G05 must retain `LOYALTY_AWARD_EVIDENCE_MISSING` and
`LOYALTY_REVERSAL_EVIDENCE_MISSING`, with `reconciled=false`. Monetary liability,
actual reward cost and loyalty-inclusive net margin stay null. Native campaign
and measurement outputs preserve their original denominators, incomplete coverage,
estimated-versus-observed cost and attribution qualifications. They are not
rewritten into a claim of causal or loyalty-inclusive profit.

There is no new points-to-money, debt, charge or store-credit instrument. No
loyalty discrepancy blocks an otherwise authorized financial refund. Unknown
redemption reconciliation uses the original operation lookup without replaying
the command. G03 refuses unexplained balance changes after its account anchor;
fresh snapshot publication is not a reconciliation shortcut.

## Required evidence still absent

The canonical M19 entry gates require accepted M07 and M09 dependencies; the
current accepted ledger supplies neither. Reused privacy, messaging, financial,
exchange and recovery obligations retain their original formal status. Local
compositions do not accept those predecessors retroactively.

A qualified merchant-owned integration still needs attributable supported accrual,
redemption and reversal observations, documented unsupported states, duplicate
and lost-acknowledgment handling, complete remaining-balance reconciliation and
explicit provider retention/deletion coverage. Source fixture success does not
establish provider API availability or a commercial relationship.

Loyalty outreach still requires qualified reused messaging and offer paths,
actual supported channel/customer behavior and the necessary consent and action
authority. The local bound recovery path is human-only and uses the original
ten-second quote; immutable expiry is an availability limit, not permission to
refresh approved terms. Enrollment is not implemented by comparison selection.

The full applicable current regression and original M19 exit demonstration are
not run as part of this focused development increment. Actual target-host,
connected provider, independent client/channel and intended-use evidence remain
open. No G99 case, release gate, deployment or formal acceptance is inferred from
source review or archive verification.

## Privacy and operational limits

Canonical G03 proof-bearing quotes and recovery rows use their existing export,
hold, minimization and restore owners. New G05 reports are transient. Retained
reports accept only already-minimized operations with current analytics/privacy
and original-resource permissions; quarantine can deny the analytics path.

Provider databases, old backups, exports and caller-held results have separate
retention boundaries. An erased core record does not erase those copies. A
reservation minimized without a configured source binding conservatively retains
its quantity and currently has no administrative release route. Larger or live
deployments also need bounded provider retrieval, representative operational
volume and physical deployment evidence beyond these disposable sources.

## Verification policy for this increment

The owner's instruction is to keep test execution minimal in favor of progress.
ADR-0061 therefore selects 14 independent new cases plus the 24 unchanged native
marketing cases in one coherent two-file batch. Additional runs require an actual
failure or material changed candidate. One compact isolated fault campaign tests
mandatory final-send validation and refund-success false reconciliation with
unchanged independent expectations. No broad suite or repeated restore campaign
follows merely from writing this assessment.

The source/test/capability inventory is additive. Existing assertions, native
IDs, protected paths, catalog entries, schema, privacy surface IDs, permissions
and worker grants remain protected. Exact candidate reports and the two
nonauthor reviews determine bounded local closure, while all formal claims above
stay blocked until their own evidence and decision exist.
