# M10 qualification assessment

**M10-G99 remains blocked.** ADR-0039 assigns local development for all fourteen
worker goals and this assessment after the reviewed M09 checkpoint. The accepted
ledger and original goal packets remain unchanged. No local fixture can provide
the missing formal M07 prerequisite or authenticate external commercial rights.

## Original milestone obligations

The milestone is warranty, dispute and cross-border workflows. Its required
demonstration is to create a warranty case and draft a dispute response, simulate
an international partner outage, and show a safe handoff. Both original exit
criteria apply together:

1. Every submitted claim/evidence package is attributable to records and
   deadlines, with accurate approvals and financial reconciliation.
2. A partner outage, unsupported jurisdiction or missing agreement prevents the
   dependent service; no guarantee or successful recovery is fabricated.

Formal acceptance requires accepted M10-G01 through G14 and M07-G99, a current
integrated candidate, its full applicable accepted regression suite, affected
connected and partner qualification, two independent reviews and a separate
trusted acceptance decision. The distinct local M10-PREP-INTEGRATION-01 case
observes the synthetic journey. It does not execute the original formal G99
cases or satisfy external evidence requirements.

## Shared ownership and privacy

ResolutionExecution, FulfillmentExecution, InstantExchanges and warehouse return
events remain the owners of their existing financial and physical observations.
Durable source bindings deny a newly constructed adapter permission to execute
a bound warranty/trial command without its current final guard. Lookup of an
already committed original effect remains a separate operation. Reconciliation
cannot rekey an unknown obligation or convert missing observations into success.

The 25 new tables have explicit tenant/store scope and subject/resource scope
where applicable. Canonical export omits replay credentials and other subjects.
Erasure rebuilds explicit allowlists, including hash-only and already minimized
restored records. Original uncertain effect identities remain audit material;
readiness, approval and consent do not survive import, restore or uninstall as
execution rights. Separate source databases, returned objects, copied exports
and historical checkpoints retain their named cleanup and physical-erasure limits.

The prior M08 limitation remains: erasure before an unknown committed inventory
movement is imported can leave shared stock unresolved. M10 adds no automatic
post-erasure stock repair; the original source identity and operator duty remain.

## Evidence scope and blocking obligations

Development retains a maximum of 60 actual native cases, four whole files and
one total 60-second deadline per invocation. The planned selection is 22 partner,
17 claims, six trial and nine retained privacy cases. Tests and independent
oracles are committed before implementation. Authors and reviewers run no
duplicate product suites or demos. Root records the integrated native run and
repeats only affected/not-yet-run whole files for a concrete remaining failure or
consequential correction. Every actual attempt stays in the checkpoint.

The named worker faults alter actual isolated modules and assert independent
behavioral outcomes. PORT-028 exercises the actual unavailable partner paths;
PORT-029 retains the attempted fabrication of a delivery acknowledgment. Their
local evidence does not provide external service qualification. All connected
and external preparation uses distinct PREP IDs. CASE-M10-G99-01 through -03
remain unperformed at their formal scope.

The remaining formal blockers are accepted M07-G99 and worker prerequisites;
real approved provider/account/interface evidence for G03/G04/G06/G13/G14;
actual access, agreements, responsibility and applicable qualification for
G07–G12; and the original current qualified integration campaign and trusted
acceptance decision. Local independent reviews authorize only their explicitly
bounded development disposition. A blocked assessment does not trigger a full
milestone campaign or silently waive a required capability.

The original no-insurer/no-customs-guarantee/no-physical-network software boundary
remains. Contacts, contracts, real customer/medical data, paid transactions,
production, hosted push/publication, tags and release are outside this assignment.
The next planning milestone is M11 inventory and procurement; it is unassigned.
