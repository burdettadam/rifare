# review-commerce-regression

Operate through the installed independent review workflow, read-only unless a separate fixing goal is assigned. Load the goal packet, protected base, accepted capability/test inventory and actual candidate evidence.

Inspect deletions, disabled routes/UI controls/flags, test skips/todos/discovery, assertion/fixture changes, capability limits, denominators, no-AI paths, migrations and current parallel work. Re-run old and new journeys; compare authoritative facts/effects, not agent summaries.

Require the goal's named deliberately broken behavior to be detected by a valid behavioral assertion. Setup/syntax/import errors do not count. Changed oracles require independent justification and a trusted rebaseline decision; do not rubber-stamp a green count.

Report reproducible findings with severity, exact IDs, commands, candidate/base and evidence. Reviewer approval is bound to actual identity/candidate through the trusted system, not a self-authored JSON flag. After consequential changes, renew the review.
